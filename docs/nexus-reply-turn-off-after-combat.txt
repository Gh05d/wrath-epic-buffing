Not annoying at all, good idea. Added in 1.22.0:

Every song and toggle (now including auras like domain and wizard auras) has two options in its detail panel:
- "Turn off after: N rounds" (the existing round limit)
- "Turn off when combat ends" (new)

You can use either or both; whichever comes first turns it off. It only switches off toggles the mod turned on itself, so anything you start by hand is left alone.
