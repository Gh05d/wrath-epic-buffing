Thanks for the report. There is no party-size limit in the mod, so what you saw was most likely a scan abort: when the mod walks the party for toggles/abilities/songs, one character whose data throws an error (typically a companion added by another mod, or a leftover ability from a mod you no longer have installed) used to stop the whole scan right there — every character after that one was simply never looked at. That is why only the first two could get Fighting Defensively: they were the ones before the broken character.

v1.21.4 scans every character (and every single ability) separately, so one broken entry can no longer hide the rest of the party. If something still refuses to show up after updating, open Player.log (AppData\LocalLow\Owlcat Games\Pathfinder Wrath Of The Righteous\Player.log) and search for "scanning activatable" — the line names the character and ability that fail; paste it here and I can look at that specific case.

Deleting the settings most likely didn't fix anything by itself (spells were already scanned per spellbook, independent of the broken character) — it just wiped your assignments, sorry about that. With 1.21.4 you should be able to set everything up once and keep it.
